Swvl Late Shift Offer Stack

One email line. Swvl helps Greater Cairo outsourced contact centers keep late-shift rides running and match transport charges to trip and boarding records in a two-week, one-route pilot without switching the whole site.

## Pilot delivery and decision

Before launch. Facilities confirms one site, one late release, one route, rider eligibility and backup. Procurement agrees the billable unit, quote, cutoff rules and sign-off path. Swvl confirms capacity, coverage, reporting access and terms.

Measure. Bill match rate is traceable billed runs divided by billed runs. Pickup punctuality is on-window scheduled pickups divided by scheduled pickups, with any agreed exclusions disclosed. Keep cancellations, no-shows and missing records visible and compare only like-for-like service.

Decision. Propose at least 95% bill match and 95% pickup punctuality for discussion. Review the actual counts and reasons at the end of the pilot, especially with a small denominator. The parties agree thresholds, exceptions and go-live in writing before launch. Verify what the buyer spends on manual reconciliation and whether the gap justifies a pilot.
