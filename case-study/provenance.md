The original workflow and workbook state that Clay, Crustdata and treg supported discovery, public sources supported scoring, and Google Drive held the deliverables. Call-level provider logs, exact queries, versions and credit charges were not retained. This repository therefore attributes that history and does not reconstruct an imaginary audit trail.

The earlier Loom prompt is superseded by prompts/codex-run.md, which assigns narrower provider jobs and requires costs and evidence to be logged. Replaying the saved data uses zero new provider credits. A fresh research run depends on each teammate's connections, access, budget and the current market.

While preparing the repository, the core Swvl service page, Egypt FAQ, 2024 shift-based transport disclosure and VXI transport role were reopened. All 25 account records were imported faithfully. Their source registry remains historical because the complete account evidence was not refreshed.

The repository adds stricter acceptance and human-review rules. The saved case stays pending under those rules. It is a reproducible research artifact with a purchase hypothesis, and commercial deployment or realized savings have not been established.
