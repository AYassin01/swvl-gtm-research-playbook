# Swvl value ladder

Segment is Greater Cairo outsourced contact centers that already arrange late-shift employee rides. The primary operator is the Facilities or Transport Manager. These are Team 03 proposals for validation with Swvl and a buyer.

| Rung | Buyer outcome | Mechanism | Commitment | Evidence needed | Bridge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free invoice check | See which charges match completed runs and which need clarification | Return a one-page matched/unmatched review before a sales call | One redacted invoice and its matching route log | Billed dates, routes, billable units and completed-run records | Proceed if a discrepancy or reconciliation burden is material enough to test |
| Paid one-route pilot | Test pickup reliability and traceability on the buyer's route | Run one scoped late-shift route, monitor pickups and reconcile its charges | Proposed two operating weeks, agreed price/targets, existing fallback as agreed, no automatic rollover | Baseline and pilot timestamps, trip records and billable units | Expand only if agreed thresholds are met and the buyer signs off |
| Managed site routes | Maintain late-shift transport with a usable monthly invoice trail | Plan routes, dispatch, live ETAs and consolidated trip reports at one site | Site contract, confirmed rosters, pickup policy and named operator | Run completion, punctuality, invoice-match rate and exception reasons | Assess a second site with a demonstrated recurring need |
| Multi-site expansion | Compare service and transport spend across sites | Roll out in waves with consolidated reporting and escalation ownership | Expanded contract, site demand and local points of contact | Each site's baseline, route demand, spending and service performance | Add sites only after scope and operating performance are agreed |

## Proposed success measures

| Measure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Pickup punctuality | Completed pickups within the agreed window divided by eligible scheduled pickups |
| Charge match rate | Billed runs with matching trip evidence divided by billed runs, using an agreed matching key |
| Invoice exception value | Value of unmatched or disputed bill lines, reported separately from confirmed overcharges |
| Reconciliation effort | Buyer time spent checking the defined invoice scope before and during the test |

The pilot period begins after agreed go-live. A two-week test can assess operational logs and a pilot invoice, while a full monthly billing comparison may need a complete cycle. Match rates are diagnostic measures. Targets, pricing, coverage and fallback obligations require explicit agreement.
