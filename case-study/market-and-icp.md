## 1 Segment

Client and offer. Swvl helps Greater Cairo outsourced contact centers keep late-shift rides running and match transport charges to trip and boarding records in a two-week, one-route pilot without switching the whole site.

Segment name. Greater Cairo outsourced contact centers with employer-arranged late-shift rides.

Why this segment. Employer ads show rides are part of overnight staffing, local job descriptions name transport vendor management, and Swvl has disclosed shift-based contracts for call-center agents in Egypt.  [iQor hiring](https://eg.linkedin.com/jobs/view/customer-service-representative-at-iqor-4481874172)  [TTEC transport](https://eg.linkedin.com/jobs/view/transportation-manager-logistics-at-ttec-4389766719)  [Swvl disclosure](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/11/15/2981875/0/en/swvl-wins-4-2-million-in-new-contracts-in-egypt-over-the-last-three-months.html)

## 2 Account criteria

| Type | Criteria | How to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firmographic | Third-party CX/BPO with an operating site in Cairo or Giza. Core scale is 250+ local staff/seats or two local delivery sites. One site can qualify for discovery if a recurring route is viable. | Clay Companies: industry/description and Egypt location. Use careers, government or operator pages for local sites and capacity. Global headcount is a discovery filter. |
| Technographic | No mandatory stack. Preference for a route schedule, rider counts and a redacted bill or run log the buyer can share. | Inspect transport job descriptions for GPS, reporting and vendor SLAs. Confirm export and billing format with Facilities. |
| Situational triggers | New client account, new Cairo site, late roster ramp or added shift, vendor renewal or tender. A transport or sourcing vacancy is a weaker signal. | Search official newsroom dates, employer job descriptions and procurement notices. Verify the buyer's decision date in discovery. |
| Disqualifiers | Remote/day-only or outside Greater Cairo, no outsourced contact center, allowance-only or self-pay for the campaign, no recurring group or a confirmed route already covered by Swvl. | Check delivery site, shift and benefit language. Confirm rider counts, approved coverage and CRM route ownership before outreach or pricing. |

Route gate. Start with a recurring release between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m. and one shared corridor. Use 8 regular riders on at least 4 nights a week as a discovery hypothesis, subject to Swvl’s vehicle economics and coverage.

## 3 Buying committee and personas

Facilities or Transport Manager · BTL champion. Owns dispatch, pickups and vendor performance. ## 4 Account scoring rubric

Score the five dimensions independently from 1 to 3. The account score is their unweighted sum out of 15. A public score reflects observable evidence as of 8 November. Company-arranged rides signal demand. A billing discrepancy requires buyer records.

| Criterion | 3 Strong | 2 Partial | 1 Weak |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fit to ICP size | 250+ Greater Cairo staff or production seats, or at least two verified operating delivery sites. | 50–249 locally, or verified local delivery with size unconfirmed. | Under 50 locally with no second delivery site, or outside the target geography. |
| Trigger present | Within 90 days, a clear Cairo site launch, client ramp, added shift, or transport review/tender. | Relevant signal 91–365 days old, or a weak signal such as hiring for a transport or sourcing role. | No qualifying signal found. Routine agent recruitment alone stays here. |
| Pain evidence | Public, specific buyer workflow around transport audit, records, performance exceptions or a stated failure. | Indirect signal such as a funded late ride, rotating shifts with rides, or an attributed recruiter claim. | No route-specific pain signal, or only an allowance for the screened campaign. |
| Reachable persona | Named relevant local owner or sponsor with an attributable public activity in the last 90 days. | Named relevant owner or referral found. Recent activity or direct route authority is unverified. | Current named local owner or credible sponsor unknown, or source conflicts unresolved. |
| No disqualifier | Public screen clean. On-site outsourced CX and company-arranged late rides evidenced, with no visible hold. | Ride terms, late window, local remit or incumbent account/route scope is unclear. | A hard stop is evidenced for the screened campaign, such as allowance-only or no local delivery. |

Routing rule. An evidenced disqualifier stops outreach even at a high score. A public Swvl logo or parent relationship goes to CRM review. Among the remaining accounts, priority discovery needs 12+, at least 2 on size and persona, a clean public screen and either a 3 on trigger or a 3 on pain evidence.

## 5 Size check

Screened sample. 25 unique operators. 16 pass the public hard-stop screen and 14 of those have a named local owner or sponsor/referral. The workbook shows 4 priority discovery, 10 discovery queue, 6 verify first, 4 CRM review and 1 excluded campaign.

Reach and market ceiling. The 14 people are public routes into accounts. Contactability, authority and private terms require confirmation. This is the Team M1 sample.
