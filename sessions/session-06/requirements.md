## What the assignment does not establish

- A matching company is not necessarily transport-qualified or ready to purchase.
- A returned title is not automatically a transport owner or a contract signer.
- A closed vacancy supports a historical campaign, not a current transport policy.
- A 25-result page is not the total market. A Clay displayed estimate can be used if labeled; a lower bound is not a completed total.
- Zero verified buyers means zero verified buyers. If the only returned profile is unconfirmed, true relevance precision remains unresolved.
- A failed hypothesis is acceptable research; missing required outputs remain incomplete.
- The repository's Module 1 five-score scheme, 15-buyer gate, and independent-release review are separate from this exercise.

## Evidence levels for the company list

Use a minimal evidence standard tied to the adopted company ICP: the firm actually provides outsourced contact-center services and has the stated operating presence. A current company or credible local business page can support that; a transport vacancy is not mandatory for every row.

Keep transport support in a separate audit as confirmed current, historical/campaign-specific, or unknown. If the owner keeps provided transport as a hard ICP criterion, then it must be supported for every included company. Do not drop an adopted criterion without recording the revised ICP and rationale.
