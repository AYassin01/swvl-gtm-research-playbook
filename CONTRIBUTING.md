# Contribute your own evidence

1. Read the setup guide and adopt a distinct hypothesis with your team.
2. Use your own authorized accounts and approved credit budget.
3. Create a unique results branch/folder. Preserve other participants' records.
4. Keep original primary results, sources, chronology and cost evidence. Explain actual unknowns.
5. Record your walkthrough, run the checks and submit a focused PR.
6. Merge the sanitized completed package through the documented workflow.

You can improve prompts, provider routing, evidence handling, the Loom guide or evaluation cases. Explain your actual contribution and keep attribution to Ahmed Yassin and GTMENA Team 03 Pipeline when using the project in a portfolio. Do not claim a template or another teammate's result as your own live research.

Original contributions use the repository MIT license. Provider data, course materials and external sources retain their respective rights.
