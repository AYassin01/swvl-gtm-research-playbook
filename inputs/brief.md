# Working brief

GTMENA Cohort 01, Team 03 Pipeline. Company case is Swvl. The original activity asks for one buyer segment, an evidence-backed pain, desired outcome, all four value levers, three obstacles with solutions, delivery, realistic risk reduction and a one-line offer.

The expanded team exercise produced market research, an ICP, an offer stack and 25 scored accounts. This repository preserves those outputs as a historical case and provides a repeatable process for future versions.

## Required outputs

- Market alternatives and sourced buyer pains.
- ICP with local fit, triggers, disqualifiers and buying committee.
- Offer stack with dream outcome, likelihood, time delay and effort.
- Three buyer obstacles paired with concrete offer elements.
- Delivery method, proposed pilot boundaries, risk reducer and honest reason to act.
- Four-step value ladder with outcome, mechanism, commitment, evidence and bridge for every step.
- Twenty-five real account records, scored independently on five criteria, with a source and rationale for each criterion.
- Evaluation report, tool-use ledger, cost reservations/charges and unresolved assumptions.

## Supplied scoring rubric

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fit to ICP size | Outside | Edge | Core |
| Trigger present | None | Weak | Recent and clear |
| Pain evidence | None | Indirect | Public and specific |
| Reachable persona | Unknown | Found | Found and active |
| No disqualifier | Hits one | Unclear | Clean |

The teaching note asks for a useful spread of scores. If every account receives 3, review whether the criteria are too loose. The team's operational thresholds are documented in [scoring](../docs/scoring.md).

## Boundaries

Use one specific segment and one primary operator. Keep claims, proof, guarantees, deadlines and pricing realistic. Label assumptions. Public transport demand and a transport-management role do not establish a billing discrepancy. A paid one-route pilot is a proposal for Swvl and buyer agreement.

The course's original templates are linked in [starting sources](sources.md). The Markdown output structures in this repository are team adaptations for replication.
