# Team roadmap

Use issues and small PRs to assign work. Contributor names and deadlines are chosen by the team.

| Priority | Work item | Definition of done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Refresh the four priority accounts | Reopen current local-policy, trigger and buyer sources. Recalculate scores and preserve the historical baseline |
| 1 | Reach the 15-buyer validation threshold | At least 15 clean accounts have named current local buyer routes with sources and access dates |
| 1 | Complete an independent evidence review | A different teammate checks all claims required by docs/evaluations.md and records findings |
| 2 | Validate purchase materiality | Permissioned buyer discovery establishes reconciliation effort or service exceptions, approval path and decision timing |
| 2 | Confirm Swvl pilot deliverability | Swvl and buyer confirm coverage, report fields, matching keys, vehicle economics, fallback and commercial terms |
| 2 | Compare the buyer's current alternative | Document switching conditions and the incumbent's actual reporting capability |
| 3 | Improve cache and provider-cost evidence | New runs show actual calls, reuse, reservations and settled charges where available |
| 3 | Extend the case through a reviewed experiment | Record an agreed pilot and measured results only when the experiment actually happens |

The first three items improve research quality. Later items require authorized discovery and cooperation from the buyer and Swvl. Open tasks describe future work and do not imply any company has agreed to participate.
