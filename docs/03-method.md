# 3. Research method and task requirements

## Why this Swvl segment

The project focuses on outsourced contact centers in Greater Cairo. Employer job advertisements can reveal late-shift transport demand, public company pages identify operating sites, and transport-management roles reveal a buyer workflow. Swvl has published shift-based call-center contract evidence. These are reasons to investigate, not proof of a billing problem or a signed purchase case at each account.

Starting references for live inspection:

- [Swvl company disclosure, 15 November 2024](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/11/15/2981875/0/en/swvl-wins-4-2-million-in-new-contracts-in-egypt-over-the-last-three-months.html)
- [Swvl Egypt FAQs](https://www.swvl.com/eg/faqs)
- [Public transport-supervisor role reference](https://eg.linkedin.com/jobs/view/transportation-supervisor-at-vxi-global-solutions-4431814434)

Reopen sources and record what is accessible, its date and exact supported claim. A closed vacancy is historical campaign evidence. Do not infer a current vacancy, person or transport policy from an old URL.

## Company mapping versus purchase qualification

For the proposed class ICP, verify outsourced contact-center services and a Greater Cairo operating site. Exclude recruitment-only firms, software-only vendors, and businesses without relevant operating presence. Global headquarters and global headcount do not establish local presence or local site size.

Track transport policy, night-shift campaigns, invoice gaps, current vendor, route demand and commercial authority separately. If a criterion becomes part of the adopted ICP, disclose and apply it consistently. No invented company, source, profile link, urgency or guarantee.

## Market sizes

Use three actual nested Clay searches with consistent counting units. Suggested study boundaries: TAM = Egypt contact-center operators; SAM = the Greater Cairo operating subset; SOM = the selected companies you can work on over 30 days, normally 25 if enough match. Explain the Egypt study boundary rather than claiming a global Swvl TAM.

Record the filters, search ID/reference, observed count, exact/estimate/cap label and observation time. Numeric ordering is necessary but does not prove logical nesting. Use the browser to inspect displayed totals when query tools only return pages; reproduce equivalent filters. Do not turn a row limit into a total.

## Buyer experiment

Choose one title family and a numerical rule before retrieval. Freeze five companies with a neutral selection method, normally alphabetical from the matching SOM. The owner writes the exact request with five domains, titles, two people each, the four professional fields, local remit, "do not guess", and no emails/phones.

Keep the original ten-request experiment separate from later supplementary research. Classify actual returns as relevant, not_relevant or unconfirmed. A relevant buyer needs support for current employer, local remit and employee-transport responsibility. Signature authority is separate. Missing results stay missing. Inaccessible evidence is unconfirmed, not proof of error.

Report companies returning people, distinct people found, verified relevant people, verified/returned rate and verified/requested yield. When there are unconfirmed people, true precision is unresolved. Apply the original rule even if it fails. Preserve a separate preregistration for any additional experiment.
